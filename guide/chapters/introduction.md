# Introduction

Astryx is an astrophotography planning tool. It helps you find deep-sky
objects worth imaging from your location, decide when and in what order to
image them, keep a log of your imaging, and analyze the ASIAir and PHD2 logs
from your sessions.

Astryx requires no internet connection and is designed for use in the
field. It runs in any modern browser, from the hosted version at
[astryx.tools](https://astryx.tools), or as a desktop app.

## About This Guide

The interactive tutorials, under **Tutorials** in the menu, walk you through
each view step by step. This guide explains what happens behind the views:
how targets are scored and filtered, how visibility is worked out, and how
plans and reports are built.

- [Target Database](target-database.md) — the target database, its
  statistics, and how to use filters effectively.
- [Best Month Calculation](best-months.md) — how Astryx determines the best
  month to observe each target, and what affects that calculation.
- [Yearly Observability](yearly-observability.md) — how to read the yearly
  observability chart and use it to plan your imaging.
- [Sequence Planner](sequence-planner.md) — planning a night's imaging with
  one or more targets.
- [Combined Session Analysis Report](combined-session-report.md) — analysis,
  summary, and recommendations from your ASIAir session and guiding logs.
